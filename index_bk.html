Jianfeng Zhang

I am a first year PhD student in the department of Electrical and Computer Engineering at the National University of Singapore. I am a member of Learning and Vision lab and my advisor is Prof. Jiashi Feng. Before that, I studied Applied Mathematics at WHU, where I worked with Prof. Xiaoping Zhang. Currently I am interested in 3D human pose/mesh estimation and related applications. Always open to cooperation opportunities. If you're interested, don't hesitate to contact me.
