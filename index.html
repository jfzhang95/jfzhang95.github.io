Jianfeng Zhang

I am a third year PhD student in the department of Electrical and Computer Engineering at National University of Singapore. I am a member of Learning and Vision Lab, supervised by Prof. Jiashi Feng and Prof. Xinchao Wang. Before that, I obtained my bachelor degree from School of Mathematics and Statistics at Wuhan University. I also spent wonderful time at Meta Reality Labs, ByteDance and Sea AI Lab.

My research focuses on 3D Human Digitization, including virtual human character, photo-realistic rendering, human pose estimation and their related application. I am open to discussion or collaboration. Please drop me an email if you're interested.

I'm expected to graduate in 2023 and looking for a job now. Please feel free to reach out if you have potential job opportunities.
