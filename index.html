Jianfeng Zhang

I am a second year PhD student in the department of Electrical and Computer Engineering at the National University of Singapore. I am a member of Learning and Vision Lab and my advisor is Prof. Jiashi Feng. Before that, I did my undergraduate studies at Wuhan University, where I worked with Prof. Xiaoping Zhang.

My research focuses on 3D Human Digitization, including virtual human character, photo-realistic rendering, human pose estimation and their related application. I am open to discussion or collaboration. Please drop me an email if you're interested.
