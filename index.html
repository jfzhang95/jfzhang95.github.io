Biography

I am a Research Scientist at ByteDance Seed, where I lead the Seed3D project. Our goal is to build World Models that simulate the real world through generative 3D foundation models, including its geometry, appearance, dynamics, and physics. We are always looking for great talents (both full-time & intern). Feel free to reach out if interested!

Beyond research, I am passionate about creating real-world impact. Over the years, technologies I developed have been deployed in production, powering 3D generation tools on the Volcano Engine and Douyin AR Platform, as well as TikTok AI Dancing.
I received my PhD from National University of Singapore, advised by Prof. Jiashi Feng. During my PhD, I interned at ByteDance AI Lab with Changhu Wang, Sea AI Lab with Prof. Shuicheng Yan, and Meta Reality Labs with Chen Cao and Weipeng Xu.
