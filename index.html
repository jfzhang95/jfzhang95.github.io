Jianfeng Zhang 张健锋

Research Scientist at ByteDance Seed  ·  Lead of Seed3D  ·  Generative 3D & World Models

I am a Research Scientist at ByteDance Seed, where I lead Seed3D. Like humans, machines will need a world to grow up in: a place to see, act, and fail safely before they enter ours. We build generative 3D world models that create such worlds, from objects to scenes to interactions, with the geometry, materials, dynamics, and physics to be acted in rather than just looked at.

Before this, I received my PhD from the National University of Singapore, with research internships at ByteDance AI Lab, Sea AI Lab, and Meta Reality Labs.

We are always looking for great talent (both full-time & intern). Feel free to reach out if interested!

Research Focus

Leading the Seed3D Project

Seed3D is our family of generative 3D foundation models. Seed3D 1.0 creates high-fidelity 3D assets with production-grade geometry and PBR materials; Seed3D 2.0 extends to part decomposition, articulation, and scene generation. The models power 3D generation on Volcano Engine, and are our first step toward worlds that machines can act in.
