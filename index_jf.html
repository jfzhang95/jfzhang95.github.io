Biography

I am a Research Scientist at ByteDance, focusing on 3D generative models and human avatar generation/animation. My research interests span computer vision, computer graphics, and deep learning, with a particular emphasis on 3D human pose estimation, 3D human reconstruction, avatar generation, and human animation.

I received my PhD from National University of Singapore in 2023, where I was fortunate to work with Prof. Jiashi Feng and Prof. Xinchao Wang. Prior to that, I completed my bachelor's degree at Wuhan University in 2018. During my academic journey, I've had the privilege of gaining industry experience through internships at Meta Reality Labs, TikTok, and Sea AI Lab.

My research has been published in top-tier conferences and journals in computer vision and machine learning, including CVPR, ICCV, NeurIPS, ECCV, ICLR, and TPAMI. I'm particularly proud of our work on PoseAug, which was recognized as a Best Paper Award Finalist at CVPR 2021, and PoseTriplet, which received an Oral Presentation at CVPR 2022.
